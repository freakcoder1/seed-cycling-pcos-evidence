## H1
Seed Cycling for PCOS: What the Evidence Actually Says

## Meta Description
Seed cycling claims to balance hormones using flax, pumpkin, sesame, and sunflower seeds. What does the research say about seed cycling for PCOS, and should you try it?

## Quick Answer
Seed cycling is a dietary strategy that rotates four seeds through the menstrual cycle. A 2025 systematic review found it may improve menstrual regularity and hormonal markers in women with PCOS "” but the evidence is based on small studies, and there are no good trials showing it balances hormones on its own.

## Body

### What Is Seed Cycling?
Seed cycling means eating flax and pumpkin seeds during the first half of your menstrual cycle (the follicular phase), then switching to sesame and sunflower seeds for the second half (the luteal phase). The idea is that each seed provides different plant compounds at different times to support hormonal balance.

### The Four Seeds
- **Flaxseed:** Rich in lignans (plant compounds with weak estrogenic activity) and omega-3 fats
- **Pumpkin seed:** Rich in zinc, which supports hormone synthesis
- **Sesame seed:** Contains lignans and may support progesterone
- **Sunflower seed:** Rich in vitamin E and selenium, which support antioxidant defense

### What the Research Says
A 2025 systematic review published in Cureus evaluated 10 studies (n=635 women) on seed cycling for PMS and PCOS. The review found that seed cycling, particularly flax and sesame seeds, was associated with:

- Improved menstrual regularity
- Reduced PMS symptom severity
- Favorable modulation of sex hormone levels
- Improvements in metabolic profiles

However, the review noted that the evidence is based on small sample sizes and moderate-quality studies. Larger randomized controlled trials are needed.

A 2026 review in PMC on functional foods for PCOS found that pumpkin seeds, flaxseed, sunflower, and sesame seeds showed promising effects when used together. In one 12-week clinical trial, women with PCOS who received 15g of each seed powder daily experienced improved hormonal profiles, insulin sensitivity, and ovarian morphology.

### What Seed Cycling Cannot Do
- There is no evidence seed cycling balances hormones on its own
- It is not a treatment for PCOS, irregular periods, or infertility
- It does not replace medical care or prescribed treatments
- The seeds themselves are nutritious "” eat them because they are good food, not as a treatment

### How to Practice Seed Cycling
- **Days 1-14:** 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed + 1 tablespoon pumpkin seeds
- **Days 15-28:** 1 tablespoon sesame seeds + 1 tablespoon sunflower seeds
- Grind flaxseed so your body can absorb its nutrients
- One to two tablespoons of mixed seeds a day is a sensible amount
